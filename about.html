How It Started

A neighborhood that needed a real bike shop.

Sheepshead Bay had no bike shop. Plenty of riders, plenty of beach-bound paths, no place to fix a flat. So in 2020 we opened the doors on Emmons Ave with a simple promise: tell people the truth about their bikes, charge fair prices, and turn jobs around fast.

That's still the whole playbook. We don't upsell tune-ups people don't need. We don't quote a $200 fix when $40 will do. And we don't keep your bike for a week when it's a 30-minute job.

Five years in, we've earned 200+ five-star Google reviews from neighbors, commuters, weekend warriors, and a lot of kids learning to ride. We're proud of that, and we work hard to keep earning it.

Our Online Arm

Meet MachX Cycles.

Customers kept asking us to sell pre-owned bikes online. So we built it. MachX Cycles is the online pre-owned division of Brooklyn Bikery — every bike is sourced, inspected, and tuned right here on the shop floor by the same mechanics who service the neighborhood. Then it ships nationwide.
