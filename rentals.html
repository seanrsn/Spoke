Forget rusty rentals. Our Breezer bikes are carefully spec'd with 8-speed drivetrains, comfortable upright geometry, and reliable components — so you actually enjoy the ride instead of fighting the bike.

Where to Ride

Trip ideas, straight from the shop.

All reachable from our front door via the Shore Greenway / Jamaica Bay path. No traffic, just water and salt air.

Coney Island

~30 min · easy

Boardwalk, Nathan's, the Wonder Wheel. The classic Brooklyn ride. Mostly flat path along the shore.

Riis Park Beach

~45 min · moderate

Cross the Marine Park bridge for the prettiest stretch of beach in NYC. Lifeguards, food trucks, art-deco bathhouse.

Floyd Bennett Field

~25 min · easy

NYC's first municipal airport — now wide-open runways perfect for cruising. Bring a picnic.

Fort Tilden

~50 min · moderate

Quiet beach, abandoned military bunkers, art installations. Way less crowded than Riis right next door.

Rockaway Beach

~55 min · moderate

Surf scene, taco shops, the Rockaway boardwalk. Earn your beach day on the bike, then take the ferry back if you're toast.

Plumb Beach

~10 min · easy

Tidal flats, horseshoe crabs, panoramic Verrazzano views. A short ride if you only have an hour.

FAQ

Good to know.

Do I need to reserve in advance? +
Same-day is usually fine on weekdays. Weekend mornings (especially in summer) — call ahead to lock in a bike and a frame size.
What if I get a flat or something breaks? +
Give us a call. We'll walk you through it over the phone — and if you need more help, we'll figure something out. We're literally right here.
Is there a deposit? +
Yes — a refundable deposit per bike. You get it back in full when the bike comes back in good shape. Call for current deposit amounts.
How long can I have it? +
Hourly, half-day, full-day, or multi-day — call for current rates. Bike just needs to be back before close on your final day.
Kid bikes? E-bikes? Tag-alongs? +
Adult Breezers are our standard rental fleet. For other sizes or styles, give us a call — we'll see what we can pull from the shop.
