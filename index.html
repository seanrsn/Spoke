🔧 Repairs

We tune and repair any and all kinds of bikes — road bikes, mountain bikes, e-bikes, fixies, cruisers, and everything in between.

Whether it's a simple flat tire, brake adjustment, or a complete overhaul, we've got you covered. Our experienced mechanics will get you back on the road quickly.

Common services: Flat repairs, brake adjustments, gear tuning, chain replacement, wheel truing, cable replacement, bearing services, and full tune-ups starting at $99.

Stop by or give us a call for a quote on your specific repair needs!

🏷️ Sales

We sell a variety of new and used bicycles in our shop. From commuter bikes to cruisers, we've got options for every type of rider.

Our inventory changes regularly as we get new bikes in and sell others, so we recommend stopping by to see what's currently available.

Why buy from us? Every bike we sell is inspected and tuned by our mechanics. Plus, you get to support a local Brooklyn business!

Looking for something specific? Give us a call and we'll let you know if we have it or can help you find it.

🎓 Lessons

We offer bike lessons for kids and adults! Whether you're learning for the first time or want to build confidence, we're here to help.

We've taught many riders of all ages — from young children taking their first pedal strokes to adults who never had the chance to learn. There's no shame in starting late!

Patient, supportive instruction in a safe environment. We go at your pace until you feel comfortable and confident on two wheels.

Call (718) 218-4464 to schedule a lesson!

🚲 Rentals & Tours

We rent high-quality Breezer bikes with 8 speeds — carefully chosen parts ensure you arrive wherever you go in style and comfort.

We're located right next to an amazing bike path stretching all the way to Queens! It's a scenic, enjoyable ride perfect for a day out.

Self-guided tours available! We provide maps and personalized recommendations based on where you want to go.

Popular destinations:

🏖️ Coney Island 🌊 Riis Park ✈️ Floyd Bennett Field 🏰 Fort Tilden 🌅 Rockaway 🦀 Plumb Beach
