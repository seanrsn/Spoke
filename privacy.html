Privacy Policy

Brooklyn Bikery · 3149 Emmons Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11235

Brooklyn Bikery respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you opt in to receive SMS messages from us.

What We Collect

When you submit our service form, we collect your name, phone number, and any service notes you provide. This information is used solely to provide bike repair services and communicate with you about your order.

How We Use Your Information

We use your phone number to send SMS messages related to your service appointments, order updates, and promotional offers you've opted into. We do not use your information for any other purpose.

We do not sell, rent, or share your SMS opt-in data or consent with any third parties.

Opting Out

You can stop receiving messages at any time by replying STOP to any text message. After opting out, you'll receive a confirmation and won't receive further messages from us.

Getting Help

If you need assistance, reply HELP to any message or contact us directly.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. However, no transmission over the internet or wireless network can be guaranteed 100% secure.
